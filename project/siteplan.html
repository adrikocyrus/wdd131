Local Legends Guide

locallegends.city

Why this name

Why this name: "Local Legends" evokes the idea of uncovering hidden gems and iconic local spots that only insiders know about. The name is memorable, aspirational, and suggests a curated, trustworthy guide. It positions the site as the definitive source for authentic local experiences. The logo (a map pin with a compass) reinforces the exploration and discovery theme.

📌 Site Purpose

The site serves as a hyperlocal culture map that celebrates the unique character of the community by providing:

  • Curated hidden gems – Showcasing 4 must-visit restaurants, cafes, and food trucks with signature dishes, descriptions, and star ratings to help visitors discover authentic local flavors
  • Non-food discoveries – Highlighting quirky bookstores, scenic parks, vintage arcades, and local theaters that give the area its distinct character
  • Community connection – Offering a simple events calendar with upcoming local happenings, from farmers markets to live music
  • Personalized recommendations – Featuring an interactive "Recommendation Quiz" that matches visitors to spots based on their mood (Adventurous, Cozy, or Healthy) using JavaScript logic
  • Visual storytelling – Creating a visually-driven lifestyle hub with images, cards, and creative typography that celebrates local culture and encourages exploration

🧭 Scenarios (visitor questions)

  • I'm visiting for the weekend — what are the absolute must-try restaurants that locals actually go to?
  • Where can I find a cozy coffee shop with good wifi to work for a few hours?
  • What cool events are happening this week that I can check out?
  • I'm feeling adventurous today — what unique spots should I explore?
  • Where's the best place to take photos for my social media?
  • Are there any family-friendly spots that my kids would enjoy?

🔤 Typography

Playfair Display (Headings & Hero)

Usage: All major headings (h1, h2, h3, h4), hero section titles, logo text, and featured spot titles. This serif font adds an editorial, sophisticated feel that matches the "local legends" premium brand identity.

Weights: 700 (bold), 900 (black), 700 italic

Inter (Body Text & UI Elements)

Usage: All body paragraphs, navigation menus, card descriptions, button text, event listings, quiz interface, and footer content. This clean sans-serif font ensures excellent readability across all devices.

Weights: 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 600 (semi-bold), 700 (bold), 800 (extra-bold)

Italic style for quotes & special callouts

Usage: Testimonials, featured quotes, special announcements, and highlighted recommendations.

📝 Summary: The combination of Playfair Display (serif) for headings and Inter (sans-serif) for body text creates a sophisticated editorial feel that's perfect for a lifestyle/culture guide. This pairing is both modern and timeless, establishing the site as a trusted, premium local resource.
